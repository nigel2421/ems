# Research desk

Studies follow Draft → Open → Closed. Questions are frozen once saved; corrections use a new study. No example responses are created.

Simple random sampling requires a complete list of anonymous frame codes. The server draws without replacement. Field workers collect only sampled codes, once. Voluntary feedback is explicitly non-probability.

Analysis shows bases, missing answers, exclusions, group filters and 95% Wilson intervals for random samples. Intervals do not address coverage or nonresponse. Reports are unweighted. Multiple-choice percentages can exceed 100%.

Export CSV for further analysis; download HTML for printing. Reports include collection method and limitations. Do not place names or phone numbers in respondent codes.
